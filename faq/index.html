Reward Details
How do I claim my reward?
Complete the eligibility check, pick your preferred payout method, and confirm your withdrawal. Rewards arrive instantly in most cases.
Which payout methods are supported?
PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, and Bank Transfer (ACH). Mobile top-up with Verizon, AT&T, or T-Mobile requires a minimum balance of $10.
How long does the payout take?
Most payouts arrive instantly. Bank transfers (ACH) may take 1-2 business days depending on your institution.
Why is there a withdrawal verification?
A one-time verification helps us confirm your identity and protect your account from unauthorized withdrawals. It is fully refundable within 24h if your reward is not released.
What are the eligibility requirements?
You must have a verified account, meet the minimum balance of $10, and be located in a supported country or region.
Who processes the payouts?
Payouts are processed by TikTok Inc. (a ByteDance company). EIN: 46-4728543.
